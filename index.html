⚠️ Important

MindMate is designed for cognitive exercise and performance tracking. It does not diagnose Alzheimer's disease or dementia. A noticeable or persistent change in memory or thinking should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why MindMate?

Making cognitive activity simpler, friendlier and more engaging for older adults.

🧠

The Idea

MindMate combines simple brain games with progress tracking. Instead of presenting cognitive exercises like a medical test, the goal is to make them feel like a friendly daily activity.

🧠

Memory

Exercises that involve remembering information.

🎯

Attention

Exercises that require concentration and focus.

📈

Tracking

Performance is stored locally to show progress.

❤️

Caregiver Support

Families can review activity and performance trends.

Medical Disclaimer

MindMate is a prototype for cognitive wellness, engagement and performance tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent or predict dementia or Alzheimer's disease.
